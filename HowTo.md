# HowTo Resources

## U.S. Treasuries

Every week, there are auctions of treasury bills of various durations according to [this published schedule](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/auctions/announcements-data-results/)

You can find the CUSIP number (a unique ID for fixed income securities) for your desired treasury length from above and
place an order from it on your favorite exchange / broker.

## FINRA Marketplace for Fixed Income

You can list and research bonds (both corporate and municipal) at the [FINRA Marketplace](https://www.finra.org/finra-data/fixed-income/corp-and-agency/trade).
